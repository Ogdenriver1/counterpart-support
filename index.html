What Counterpart Business does

Counterpart Business gives two businesses that work together, like a supplier and a café or a designer and a client, one shared place for their working relationship. Each business signs in, creates a profile, and connects with the other using an invite code. Then they can message, send and track invoices, sign proposals and contracts, and schedule meetings, all inside the app.

How connecting works

  1. Sign in with Apple or Google and enter your business name.
  2. Share your code. Send your 6-character invite code to a business you work with, or enter theirs.
  3. Work together. Everything you share is visible to both of you, and no one else.

How Counterpart Business uses Google sign-in

You can sign in with Google (or Apple) instead of creating a password. When you do, Counterpart Business receives only your basic Google profile: your name, email address and profile picture. We use them solely to create your account, pre-fill your business profile and keep you signed in. We don't access your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data, and we never post anything to your Google account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Help

How do I connect with a business?

Open the Businesses tab and tap +. Share your invite link or code with them, or type in the 6-character code they sent you.

Can the other business see my notes and tasks?

No. Notes, tasks and pipeline stages are private to you. Messages, invoices, meetings, proposals and contracts are shared with that business.

Does Counterpart take payments?

No. Counterpart never moves money. You record payments on an invoice (cash, bank transfer, card and so on) so both sides can see what's been paid.

Someone is bothering me. What can I do?

Open their page, tap ⋯, and choose Report or Block. Blocking disconnects you and stops them connecting again. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, connections, messages, invoices, meetings, documents, notes and tasks.
